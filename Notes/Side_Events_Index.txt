CFCCA side-event and non-slide-course-material index
Last updated: 2026-05-19

Purpose
This file lists lecture material that should remain traceable, but should not be treated as ordinary slide-backed chapter content unless explicitly stated. These items may appear in the per-lecture notes because they happened during the lecture. Student presentations, homework feedback, administrative explanations, and guest/professional digressions should generally not be merged into Chapters_MD topic notes.

Chapter-note rule
- Keep in Lectures_MD: chronological record of what happened in class.
- Keep in this index: references to side events, presentations, labs, and administrative material.
- Keep out of Chapters_MD: student presentations, homework feedback, exam logistics, informal administrative notes, and non-slide guest/professional discussions.
- Exception: if a side event has an official slide deck or the user explicitly wants a dedicated note, place the full side-event note under Side_Events_MD rather than Chapters_MD.

Confirmed isolation status
- Explicit student presentations are currently isolated from Chapters_MD.
- The Paolo Dal Checco / Garlasco guest discussion was moved out of Chapters_MD and into Side_Events_MD.
- Lab/course exercises that are part of an official slide deck remain in Chapters_MD only when they directly explain the slide topic.
